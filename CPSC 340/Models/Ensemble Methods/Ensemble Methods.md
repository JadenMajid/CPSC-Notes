# Description
Ensemble methods are a category of [[Supervised Learning|Supervised]] [[Classifier]] models that train sub-models, and use their output to create some final prediction. The simple canonical method is to predict the [[Mode]] of the sub-model predictions. 

Some ensemble methods train many different types of models on the data, then train some model on the submodel's predictions that attempts to learn patterns in the accuracies of the submodels.
