The p-norms are a group of functions that convert [[Vector|Vectors]] to [[Real Numbers]]. The p-Norms are [[Convex]] for $p\ge1$.
The general formula for a p norm is as follows:
$$
\|x\|_p \;=\; \left( \sum_{i=1}^n |x_i|^p \right)^{1/p}, \qquad p \ge 1.
$$
