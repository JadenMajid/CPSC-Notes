## Characteristics of Cells
1. Cell membrane
	1. Semipermeable membrane that encloses the cell and acts as a barrier between internal and external environments
2. [[Cytoplasm]]
	1. Liquid component of the cell and [[Organelles]]
3. [[Ribosomes]]
	1. Tiny structures that carry out protein synthesis(vital to cell division and repair)
4. [[DNA]]
	1. molecules that encode genetic information for the development and functioning of the cell

## Mitosis and Meiosis

#### Phases

1. Interphase(Resting Phase where a cell spends 90% of its life)
	1. G1
		1. Cell Growth
	2. Synthesis(S)
		1. Copying of DNA
	3. G2
		1. Cell growth
2. Mitosis
	1. Prophase
		1. Chromosomes condense and spindles begin to form
	2. Metaphase
		1. Chromosomes move to the centre of the cell
	3. Anaphase
		1. sister chromosomes are pulled to the opposite sides of the cell
	4. Telophase
		1. Nuclear Envelope reforms and DNA decondenses
	5. Cytokinesis
		1. cell membrane splits and reforms as two cells
