### Silicon
- Poor electrical conductor
	- 4/8 electrons in valence shell fillled
- Makes it easy to engineer electric properties
	- Add element with 5 valence electrons(phosphorus or arsenic) -> more electrons
	- Add element with 3 valence electrons(gallium or indium) -> "missing" electrons
	- Missing electrons called "holes" and act like positively charged particles

### Diode
![[Screenshot 2026-01-28 at 15.07.05.png]]
- Dope silicon to have n-doped region next to p-doped region.
- Electrons flow from n to p doped regions, but not the other way around

### Transistors
![[Screenshot 2026-01-28 at 15.08.58.png]]
- Positive voltage on the gate attracts electrons, which create an electron bridge for current to flow
- Negative voltage on the gate breaks the bridge and stops current

### Transistors -> Computers
- Once we have transistors we can make gates(AND, NAND, XOR, etc)
