## 1. Physics & Orbital Mechanics
* **Kinetic Energy:** $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
* **Impact Energy Conversion:** $1 \text{ tonne TNT} = 4.184 \times 10^9 \text{ J}$
* **Newton's Form of Kepler's 3rd:** $P^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 a^3}{G(M_1 + M_2)}$
* **Velocities:**
    * Circular: $v_c = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$
    * Escape: $v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}$
* **Ellipse Geometry:**
    * Eccentricity: $e = \frac{c}{a}$ (where $c$ is distance from center to focus)
    * Pericentre (closest): $q = a(1-e)$
    * Apocentre (farthest): $Q = a(1+e)$

## 2. Radiation & Thermodynamics
* **Wien's Law:** $\lambda_{peak} = \frac{0.0029}{T}$ (Temperature in Kelvin)
* **Stefan-Boltzmann Law:** $F = \sigma T^4$ ($\sigma \approx 5.67 \times 10^{-8} \text{ W m}^{-2} \text{ K}^{-4}$)
* **Flux-Luminosity:** $F = \frac{L}{4\pi d^2}$ (Inverse Square Law)
* **Equilibrium Temperature:** $T_{eq} = \left[ \frac{L(1-A)}{16\pi\sigma d^2 \epsilon} \right]^{1/4}$
    * $A$: Bond Albedo (0 to 1)
    * $\epsilon$: Emissivity (usually 1 for black body)

## 3. Biology & Early Earth
* **Chirality (Handedness):**
    * **Isomer:** Same atoms, different arrangement.
    * **Enantiomer:** Non-superimposable mirror images.
    * **Racemic:** 50/50 mix (non-biological). Life uses **L-amino acids**.
* **Cell Components:**
    * **Lipids:** Amphiphilic (Hydrophilic head/Hydrophobic tail).
    * **Vesicle:** Lipid bilayer sphere; precursor to cell membranes.
* **Atmospheric Stages:**
    1.  **Archean:** High $CH_4$, $CO_2$, very low $O_2$.
    2.  **Great Oxidation Event (GOE):** Rise of cyanobacteria/photosynthesis.
    3.  **BIFs:** Banded Iron Formations; evidence of oxygen reacting with dissolved iron.

## 4. Earth Systems & Cycles
* **Carbonate-Silicate Cycle:**
    * $CO_2 + \text{Rain} \rightarrow \text{Carbonic Acid} \rightarrow \text{Silicate Weathering}$.
    * Sequesters carbon in seafloor rocks; returned via subduction and volcanoes.
* **Milankovitch Cycles:**
    * **Eccentricity:** Orbit shape (~100k yrs).
    * **Obliquity:** Tilt (~41k yrs).
    * **Precession:** Rotation axis wobble (~23k yrs).
* **Tides:**
    * **Spring:** Sun and Moon aligned (New/Full Moon); highest tidal range.
    * **Neap:** Sun and Moon at 90°; lowest tidal range.

## 5. Key Terms
* **Panspermia:** Life hitchhiking on meteorites (e.g., ALH84001).
* **Chemosynthesis:** Energy from chemical reactions (e.g., $H_2S$) rather than sunlight.
* **Faint Young Sun Paradox:** Earth stayed liquid despite Sun being ~30% dimmer (likely due to greenhouse gases).

### Kepler's Laws

1.  **The Law of Orbits**: All planets move in elliptical orbits, with the Sun at one focus.
    

[Image of Kepler's first law showing an elliptical orbit with the Sun at one focus]

2.  **The Law of Areas**: A line that connects a planet to the Sun sweeps out equal areas in equal times. 
    * *Implication*: Orbital speed is non-uniform; fastest at pericentre, slowest at apocentre.
    
3.  **The Law of Periods**: The square of the orbital period ($P$) is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis ($a$).
    * Simplified (units in years/AU): $P^2 = a^3$
    * Newtonian Generalization: $P^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 a^3}{G(M_1 + M_2)}$
