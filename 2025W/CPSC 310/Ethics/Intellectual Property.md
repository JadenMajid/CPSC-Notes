All technology companies consume and generate IP in some way or another. 
# Mechanisms
- Trade secrets
	- Trade secrets are internal secrets that companies do not want released to the broader public. Things like source code can be considered trade secrets.
	- Compiled versions of programs are not considered disclosure of source code
- Trademarks
	- Trademarks are marks used by customers and companies to differentiate products in a market place. They must be registered on a country-by-country basis
- Copyright
	- can be applied to creative work
	- protects how the idea is encoded, but not the idea its self
	- in Canada, copyright lasts the lifetime of the author+50 years, then becomes public domain
	- contrasted by [[Copyleft]]
- Patents
	- patents are like trademarks in that they must be registered, but they protect the specific implementation of an invention. Patents must be proven to be non-obvious and unique
- Licenses
	- licenses clarify how code can be used and whether it can be modified
	- common types
		- GPL
		- LGPL
		- MPL
		- CC-by
