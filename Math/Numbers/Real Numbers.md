The real numbers, denoted $\mathbb{R}$ is the union of [[Rational Numbers|Rational]] $\mathbb{Q}$ and [[Irrational Numbers|Irrational]] $\mathbb{R} \textbackslash \mathbb{Q}$ numbers. The reals can be imagined as all numbers that fit on the number line.
